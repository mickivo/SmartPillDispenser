📖 View User Guide & Instructions
System Status (Top Right)
  • Online: The web portal functions normally.
  • Offline: The portal remains accessible, but data cannot be transferred to the physical SmartPill Dispenser.
Dashboard
  • Next Schedule Countdown: Displays the closest upcoming schedule, the designated compartment, and the time remaining.
  • Dispenser Compartments: Shows the real-time physical lock/unlock status of the box.
  • Emergency Unlock: Allows forced opening of a compartment. Note: The selected compartment must have an active schedule and sufficient inventory for a single dose to unlock.
  • Overall Compliance: Displays the patient's medication adherence as a percentage.
Schedule
  • Schedule Management: Create schedules for Compartment A & B (max 4 schedules per compartment). A mandatory 2-hour gap is required between schedules for the same compartment.
  • Active Schedules: Lists all currently created schedules.
  • Inventory & Refill Management: Tracks remaining pills. Use the refill button to restock. Crucial: If the inventory is empty, the active schedules will not trigger.
  • Refill History Logs: Displays a complete history of medication refills.
History
  • Patient Header: Shows the patient's name, diagnosis, and caregiver contact number for medical reference.
  • Doctor QR Access: Generates a QR code for doctors to live-monitor the patient's history page.
  • Compliance Statistics: Summarizes the total taken vs. missed doses, calculated as a percentage.
  • History Log: Provides a complete record of all medication intake events.
Settings
  • Profile: Enter and update patient and caregiver details.
  • Timer (Time Zone): Synchronizes the web and SmartPill Dispenser to the selected time zone for accurate scheduling.
  • Timer (Dispense Timeout): Adjust the duration allowed for the patient to retrieve their medication.
  • System (Doctor QR Access): Monitor active doctor sessions and revoke (kick) access if necessary.
